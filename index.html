PLAGUE DEFENSE

Pick a disease. Send immune cells where the virus attacks. Survive every wave.

How to play

Goal: survive every wave of virus, then clear what's left. You lose if the Brain, Heart or Lungs reach 0 health, or the whole body drops below 30%.

The loop

  1. Watch for the next wave. The top bar and a pulsing ring on the map show which organ gets hit next, and when.
  2. Build cells in the Bone Marrow: click it and press a button, or use keys 1–3 from anywhere. Cells cost Energy, which refills over time.
  3. Move cells: drag from one organ to another to send every cell there. Or select an organ and press Send next to a cell type, then click where to go.
  4. Cells fight on their own when they arrive. They die of old age, so keep the reinforcements coming.

Cells

Tips

  • Get there first. Cells already waiting in an organ crush a wave as it lands. Late cells face a much bigger fight.
  • Send new cells to: set this on the Bone Marrow so every new cell walks straight to that organ.
  • Memory fills as you kill virus. When it's full you can build Killer T cells.
  • Fever (F) helps in a crisis but drains Energy and hurts every organ. Turn it off afterwards.
  • Space pauses. Use it to plan.
